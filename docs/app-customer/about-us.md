# About Us Page

Control the content shown on the app's **About Us** screen.

## Add & Edit About Us Content

1. Log in to the your **Admin Panel**.
2. Navigate to **Settings → Website & Apps → About us**.
3. Enter or update the content using the **HTML editor**.
4. Click **Save**.

The app fetches this content at runtime and renders it on the About Us screen.
