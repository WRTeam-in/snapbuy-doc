# Policy Page

Control the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions shown in the app.

## Add & Edit Policy Content

1. Log in to your **Admin Panel**.
2. Navigate to **Countries** and select the country.
3. Open its **Policy** section.
4. Enter or update the content using the **HTML editor**.
5. Click **Save**.

Policy content is set **per country**, so update it for each country you operate in.

The app fetches this content at runtime and renders it on the Policy screen.
