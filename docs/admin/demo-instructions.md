## What the demo will and will not do

:::warning The demo is shared and resets periodically
Everyone evaluating SnapBuy uses the same installation. Records you create may be changed or removed by someone else, and the database is reset from time to time. Treat it as a showroom, not a sandbox you own.
:::

A few operations are limited on the shared demo so it stays usable for everyone. Third-party credentials — payment, SMS, Firebase — are either blank or point at test accounts, so payments do not settle and OTP messages are not delivered to arbitrary numbers.

:::info Your own installation has no such limits
A licensed installation on your own server runs with every feature enabled.
:::

## Suggested tour

If you have fifteen minutes and want to judge whether SnapBuy fits your business:

1. **Dashboard** — the operational overview.
2. **[Zones](/docs/admin/zones)** — open a zone and look at the two pricing models. This is where most delivery businesses succeed or fail.
3. **[Stores](/docs/admin/stores)** — note that stock and price are held per outlet.
4. **[Home Builder](/docs/admin/home-builder)** — open a layout and use the live device preview.
5. **[Roles & Permissions](/docs/admin/roles-permissions)** — check the permission granularity against your staffing.
6. **[Reports](/docs/admin/reports)** — ten reports covering sales, inventory, delivery and payment.
