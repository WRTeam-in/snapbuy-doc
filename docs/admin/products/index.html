Products

Menu path: Products → Manage Products · Products → Add Product

Everything a customer can buy lives here. A product is created through a five-step wizard, and nothing reaches the catalogue until the last step is confirmed.

The listing​

ControlWhat it does
Published / DraftsTwo tabs. A draft is a product saved part-way through the wizard — invisible to customers until it is completed and published.
Country / ZoneNarrows the list to products listed at the branches of that country or zone
FiltersCategory, brand, status, sales channel
SortLatest, name, price

Each card shows the product's status, category, sales channel, price and how many variants it has.

Price on the card is indicative

A product is priced per branch, so the card shows one representative figure. The real prices live on the Variants step, branch by branch.

Step 1 — Category​

FieldWhat it does
CategoryWhere the product sits in the catalogue tree
Attributes from categoryThe attributes this category offers — tick the ones this product varies by
Pick the last level

A category that has sub-categories is shown for context but cannot be chosen. Only a leaf category accepts products.

The category decides the attributes, and the attributes decide the variants

Attributes are inherited from the category, and the values you tick here build the variant matrix in step 3. Changing the category of a product that already has variants means rebuilding them, so get this right first.

If the category has no attributes, add them under Categories → Attributes before creating the product.

Step 2 — Product Details​

Identity​

FieldWhat it does
Product NameShown everywhere. Translatable — one name per language.
BrandOptional. Powers brand pages and the Top Brands section in Home Builder.
Tax CategoryOne classification for the product. The rate comes from each country's tax rules, so the same category is taxed differently by a branch in India and one in the UAE.
Product TypeNone, Veg, Non-veg, Chemical, Eggetarian, Medical — the diet or handling marker shown on the product card
Is this a food item?Made to order, with addons
Is this sold only as an addon?Attachable to a dish, never listed on its own
Prescription RequiredShown for a Medical product. The customer must upload a prescription (image or PDF) before the order can be placed.
ManufacturerFree text, printed on the product page
Made InCountry of origin
TagsComma-separated. They feed search and related-product matching.

Selling rules​

FieldWhat it does
Sales channelQuick commerce, eCommerce or Both. Only branches matching this channel are offered for inventory in step 3.
Total Allowed Quantity in CartThe most of this product one cart may hold. 0 means no limit.
StatusInactive products disappear from the apps immediately
The sales channel decides which branches you can stock

Set it before step 3. Switching it afterwards drops the branches that no longer match, and their pricing with them.

Descriptions​

FieldWhat it does
Short DescriptionOne or two lines under the product name
DescriptionThe full rich-text description
Custom SectionsExtra structured blocks the category defines — nutrition, storage, FSSAI licence, disclaimers

Generate with AI drafts either description from the product name and category. Treat it as a first draft and read it before saving.

Return and cancellation policy​

FieldWhat it does
Return StatusReturnable or Not returnable
Return DaysHow many days after delivery a return may be raised. Shown only when returnable.
CancelableWhether the customer may cancel at all
Cancellable TillThe order status up to which cancelling is allowed. A product sold on both channels gets one setting for Quick and one for eCommerce, because the two move at different speeds.
COD AvailableWhether cash on delivery may be used for this product
These beat the global settings for this product alone

A shop-wide return window is a default. What you set here is what the customer actually gets for this product.

Step 3 — Variants​

This is where price and stock live. A product has at least one variant, even when nothing varies.

The variant matrix​

Tick the values for the attributes this product varies by and leave the rest empty. Every combination becomes a variant — two pack sizes and three colours make six.

Generate All Variants builds them in one go; the banner tells you how many you are about to create.

Per-variant fields​

FieldWhat it does
Variant NameShown in the variant picker — Red / 0-1 yr
SKUYour own stock code. Required, and the key the bulk sheets match on.
HSN CodeThe internationally standardised goods code used for GST and invoicing — 6109 for T-shirts. Printed on invoices when set.
Weight (kg)Dead weight of one unit. Courier integrations quote and label from it.
Package Size (cm)Length × breadth × height of one packed unit, used for volumetric weight
Main ImageThe picture used on cards and listings. Recommended 800×800px.
Other ImagesThe gallery on the product page

Choose From Media picks an image already uploaded instead of uploading it again.

A variant with no weight cannot be shipped by courier

Shiprocket refuses to quote a parcel whose weight is unknown, and the ship dialog names the products that are missing one. Fill the weight for anything you post.

Branch-wise inventory​

Each card below the variant is one branch that matches the product's sales channel.

FieldWhat it does
ListedOn: customers can see and buy this SKU at this branch. Off hides it there without losing the quantities.
Purchase PriceWhat it costs you. Required — it is what the margin is calculated from.
Selling PriceThe normal price, in that branch's currency
Discounted PriceThe offer price. Leave empty for none.
Customer PriceRead-only — what the shopper actually sees, tax included if tax is added
Price includes taxOn when the price you typed already has tax in it
Estimated marginRead-only — selling minus purchase, as an amount and a percentage
Quantity-based slab pricingOptional cheaper price per quantity band: Min Qty, Max Qty, Price. Leave the last row's Max Qty blank to mean "and above". The base selling price applies when no slab matches.
Stock Limit TypeLimited tracks quantities; Unlimited never runs out
Stock statusCustomer-facing In stock / Out of stock, independent of the numbers — use it for a manual sell-out
AvailableUnits you can sell right now, not yet tied to open orders
ReservedUnits held in carts and pending orders. Managed automatically — not editable.
Min alertLow-stock threshold for this branch, used for replenishment alerts and reporting

Copy first branch to all applies the first branch's pricing and stock to every other branch.

Check every branch that prices in another currency

The copy is a straight number copy. 65 stays 65 whether that branch charges in ₹, £ or AED. Review each one afterwards.

Why there is no single global price

Pricing is held per branch and per variant, so one outlet can run a promotion while another does not, and each prices in its own currency. A product with no price at a branch is simply not sold there.

Step 4 — SEO​

FieldWhat it does
Meta TitleThe browser-tab and search-result title
Meta KeywordsComma-separated keywords
Meta DescriptionThe snippet under the search result
Schema MarkupStructured data for rich search results

Generate SEO with AI fills the three text fields from the product you have already described.

Leave it blank rather than stuffing it

An empty meta description makes search engines write their own from the page, which is usually better than a keyword list. Write one when you have something specific to say.

Step 5 — Review​

The last step lists every variant about to be created, with its SKU, pricing and the branches it will be listed at.

Nothing is saved until you press Create Products. Until then the whole wizard is a draft in your browser.

Save as Draft to come back later

Save as Draft stores an unfinished product under the Drafts tab on the listing. Customers never see a draft. It is also how you get a product id early — the Addons editor needs a saved product.

Editing an existing product​

Opening a product from the listing puts you back in the same five steps, with every step unlocked. Changes save per step, so you can edit prices without touching the description.

Adding an attribute value later and pressing Generate All Variants adds the missing combinations and leaves the existing ones alone.

Troubleshooting​

SymptomCauseFix
Category cannot be selectedIt has sub-categoriesPick a leaf category
No attributes offeredThe category has noneAdd them under Categories → Attributes
No branches on step 3None match the product's sales channelChange the channel, or set the branch's fulfilment type on Branches
Product invisible to customersNot Listed at the branch serving that zone, or Status inactiveTick Listed on that branch; check Status
Price shows but nobody can buyStock status Out of stock, or Available is 0 with Limited stockRestock, or switch Stock Limit Type to Unlimited
Courier booking refuses the itemThe variant has no WeightFill Weight (kg) on that variant
Margin looks wrongPurchase price missing or includes tax inconsistentlyRe-enter purchase price; check Price includes tax
Changes not visible in the appThe product is still a draftFinish the wizard and press Create Products

Checklist​

  • Leaf category chosen, with the right attributes ticked
  • Sales channel set before pricing
  • Tax category set — it drives the rate in every country
  • Every variant has an SKU and a main image
  • Weight and package size filled for anything shipped by courier
  • Purchase and selling price set at every branch that should sell it
  • Stock quantities and min alert set where stock is Limited
  • Return and cancellation policy matches what you can actually honour
  • Created the product and opened it once in the app
