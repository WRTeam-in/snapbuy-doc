In-App Purchases

In-App Purchases (IAP) let the Customer App sell digital products directly through Google Play and the Apple App Store — for example a premium membership tier or an ad-free unlock.

Customer App only

This feature applies to the Customer App. The Delivery App does not use in-app purchases.

1. IAP Types

TypeDescriptionExamples
ConsumableCan be purchased repeatedly; used up after purchaseIn-app currency, one-time promo boosts
Non-ConsumablePurchased once and permanently unlockedPremium membership, remove-ads unlock, theme packs

2. App Store Connect Setup (iOS)

Before creating any IAP product on iOS, complete these account-level steps once:

  • Sign the Paid Applications Agreement in App Store Connect.
  • Set up your banking and tax information under Agreements, Tax, and Banking.
  • Accept the latest Apple Developer Program License Agreement.

Approval takes time

Apple can take a few days to approve these agreements. Until approved, your IAP products will not display in the app even if the code is implemented correctly — plan this step well before your launch date.

To create a product:

  1. In App Store Connect, open your app and go to Features → In-App Purchases.
  2. Click + and choose Consumable or Non-Consumable.
  3. Enter a Reference Name and a unique Product ID (e.g. com.yourcompany.snapbuy.premium_monthly).
  4. Fill in Availability, Price Schedule, Localization (display name and description), and Review Information (including a screenshot).
  5. Save, then attach the IAP to your app's next submission under In-App Purchases in the version you submit for review.

3. Google Play Console Setup (Android)

  1. Confirm your Play Console account's country supports merchant registration.
  2. Set up a payments profile under Setup → Payments profile if you haven't already.
  3. Go to Monetize → Products → In-app products and create a product with a Product ID, Title, Description, and Price.
  4. Enable Multi-quantity if the product is consumable and can be purchased in bulk.
  5. Set the product status to Active.
  6. Publish your app to at least an internal testing or production track — draft products won't load in an unpublished app.

4. Testing

Google Play requires license testers: add tester Gmail accounts under Setup → License testing in Play Console so you can complete test purchases without being charged.

5. Troubleshooting

SymptomLikely CauseFix
IAP products don't appear in the appAgreements not yet approved, or product not ActiveWait for Apple/Google approval; confirm product status is Active/live
Sandbox purchases fail on iOSTesting with a real Apple ID instead of a Sandbox testerSign out of the App Store on the test device and sign in with a Sandbox Apple ID
Products load on Android in testing but not productionApp not published to a track, or product still in DraftPublish the app and set the product to Active
Purchase succeeds but content doesn't unlockBackend not verifying/recording the purchase receiptConfirm the receipt-verification call to your Admin Panel backend completes and updates the user's entitlement

Tip

Keep Product IDs identical in intent between Android and iOS (e.g. both named premium_monthly) even though the platforms manage them in separate consoles — it keeps your backend entitlement logic simple.
