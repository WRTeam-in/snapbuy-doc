General Settings — Flutter App

This section covers the core configuration needed to rebrand, connect, and publish the Delivery App. Apply these steps to both the Customer App and Delivery App unless a page states otherwise — the Customer App version of this section is on its own General Settings page.

Before You Begin

Complete these in order, before touching any of the settings below:

  • Install Flutter SDK, Java JDK, and an IDE and confirm flutter doctor -v passes with no errors.
  • Have your Admin Panel already installed and reachable — the app talks to it over HTTPS.
  • Use the same Firebase project as the Customer App and Admin Panel — Firebase powers authentication, push notifications, and OTP.
  • Decide your package name (e.g. com.yourcompany.snapbuydelivery) before running Firebase registration — registering under the wrong package name means redoing that step later.
  • Have your app icon, name, brand color, and legal pages (Privacy Policy / Terms) ready — you'll need them during setup.
